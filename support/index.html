About the app

3LP Junior gives a child two decisions a month — what to save, risk or spend, and how much to put at risk — and then makes them live with it until the month is out. Safe Savings grows slowly and never falls. Risky Investment does whatever that month's event and that month's challenge decide between them. It is a small app, run by one person, and the questions below are the ones that actually come up.

Common questions

Is any real money involved?

No. The balances in 3LP Junior are a record, not a wallet. No bank account or card is ever connected, nothing is paid in or taken out, and the app cannot move money anywhere. Most parents use it to mirror a real savings account they already hold for their child.

Where is our data kept?

On your device, and nowhere else. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no server of ours. The only connection the app ever makes is to the App Store, to check whether your subscription is current — nothing about your family is sent anywhere. See the privacy policy for the detail.

I am changing phones. How do I move everything across?

Open Parent Settings and choose Export a backup. Save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. Install the app there, open Parent Settings and choose Restore a backup. Your savers, balances and full month history come across.

I have forgotten the parent PIN.

There is no way to recover it — it is held in the device keychain and we never see it. If Face ID is enabled you can still get in that way. Otherwise the only route is to delete and reinstall the app, which erases everything on the device, so export a backup first if you possibly can.

Why did nothing happen when the month changed?

Months advance when the app is opened or brought back to the foreground. If your child had money in Risky Investment and did not play that month's challenge, the app waits for them rather than skipping ahead — play the challenge and the following months catch up.

My child had a bad first go at the Skill Check.

Open Parent Settings, find them under Savers, and choose Check again. It sets a fresh starting level and no money is involved. The level also adjusts on its own as they play.

How do I remove a saver, or start over?

Both live in Parent Settings, behind the PIN. Removing a saver deletes their balances and every month of their history, and there is no undo — export a backup first.

Can I change how much the challenges can pay?

Yes. Account Rules in Parent Settings sets the interest rate on Safe Savings, each saver's monthly allowance, and the worst and best a risky month can ever go. Nothing the app does can land outside those limits.

Reporting a problem

Email 3lpapps@gmail.com. It helps to include your iOS version, roughly when it happened, and what was on screen. Please do not send a backup file unless we ask — it contains your children's names and balances.
