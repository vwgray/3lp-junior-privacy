3LP Junior — Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

3LP Junior does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and the app never connects to the internet.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app records stays on your device:

  • Savers, balances and month history are kept in a file inside the app's own storage area on your device.
  • The parent PIN is kept in the device keychain, which is the secure store iOS provides for small secrets.

None of it is sent anywhere. There is no server behind this app to send it to.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, email addresses or passwords.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party software development kits that collect data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising requests — and no server of ours to send anything to. The single exception is described under Subscription below.

Children

This app is made for children to use with a parent. Because it collects no personal information at all, there is nothing gathered from a child to disclose, share or sell. That is a deliberate design decision rather than a policy promise: the app has no way to send data anywhere.

Parent Settings sit behind a PIN so that account rules and saver details are managed by an adult.

Face ID

If you choose to unlock Parent Settings with Face ID, the check is performed by iOS. The app is told only whether the check passed. Face data never reaches the app and is never stored by it.

Subscription

3LP Junior is a paid monthly subscription with a free first month. To know whether yours is current, the app asks Apple. That is the only request it makes to anyone, and it is a question about a receipt, not about you: no names, no balances and nothing from inside the app is sent, because Apple already knows what was bought and we do not need to tell it anything.

Payment is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your card, and we are never told who you are — only whether this device has an active subscription. See the terms of use for how cancelling works.

Reminders

If you switch on the monthly reminder in Parent Settings, iOS schedules a notification on your device for the first Saturday of each month. It is created and stored entirely on the phone: there is no server behind it, nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and the wording is fixed when it is scheduled rather than fetched later. The app asks for notification permission only at the moment you turn the reminder on, never at startup. Switching it off in Parent Settings removes the pending reminders, and turning notifications off in iOS Settings works too.

Backups you create

The app lets a parent export a backup file so savings history is not lost when changing phones. That file is created only when you ask for it, and where it goes is entirely your choice. Once you save it, share it or email it, it is covered by whatever service you put it in rather than by this policy.

Separately, your device's own iCloud or computer backups may include the app's stored data, in the same way they include other apps'. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple.

Deleting your data

Remove an individual saver from that saver's Edit screen, or use the wipe option in Parent Settings to clear everything. Deleting the app removes all of it from your device. Since nothing was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing else to request the deletion of.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this, the policy will be updated here and the date above will change. If a future version ever collected anything, that would be described plainly before it shipped.
