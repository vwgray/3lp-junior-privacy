3LP Junior — Terms of Use

Last updated 23 August 2026

Plain version: you pay monthly for the app to keep going, the first month is free, you can cancel whenever you like, and nothing you have saved is ever taken away from you.

What the app is

3LP Junior is a teaching tool. It holds no real money. The balances in it are a record you keep, most often mirroring a savings account you already hold for your child elsewhere. It is not a bank, not a payment service and not financial advice, and it cannot move money anywhere.

The subscription

  • The first month is free. If you cancel before it ends, you are not charged.
  • After that it renews monthly at the price shown in the app, in your local currency.
  • It renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before a period ends to avoid the next charge.
  • Payment is taken by Apple, through your Apple ID. We never see your card.
  • Manage or cancel it in Settings on your device, under your name, then Subscriptions.

What stops if you cancel

Only the monthly cycle: no new months, no new market events and no new challenges.

Your records stay. Balances, every month of history, the growth chart and the ability to export a backup all keep working whether you subscribe or not. A savings record you have kept for your child is yours, and we will not hold it hostage. Subscribe again at any time and the app picks up where it left off.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we have no ability to issue one. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something went wrong, email us anyway and we will help you make the case.

Your data

Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy for the detail. The one thing that leaves is a check with Apple to see whether the subscription is current, and that check carries nothing of yours.

Fair use

One subscription covers your household and every saver in it. Please do not try to break, copy or resell the app.

No warranty, and the limit of what we owe you

The app is provided as it is. We work hard to make it correct and we test it thoroughly, but we cannot promise it will never have a fault. Do not rely on it as the only record of money you are holding for your child — keep your own, and export a backup from time to time. If something does go wrong, our liability is limited to what you have paid us in the previous twelve months.

Changes

If these terms change, the date above changes and the new version appears here. A change to the price is never applied to an existing subscription without Apple asking you first.
